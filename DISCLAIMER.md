The short version:

  entitlement, amount and duration.
- **The figure shown is an UPPER BOUND.** Art. 29.º n.os 2–3 caps the benefit at
  75 % of the **net** reference remuneration, and net includes income-tax
  withholding this program never asks about. The real amount can be **lower**.
  Never higher. Do not commit to rent or credit on the basis of it.
- **You supply the figures; you own the decision.** A one-day error in the
  qualifying period changes the result and passes every test here. Confirm your
  real numbers in Segurança Social Direta.
- **Art. 37.º n.os 3–5 is not modelled** — a previous unemployment claim may mean
  a **shorter** duration than calculated.
